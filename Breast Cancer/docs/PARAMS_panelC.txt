Breast Panel C vs Groves Figure 1C

What Groves actually did (not “project tumors into the cell-line PCA”):
  1. Merge cell-line matrix + tumor matrix on shared genes.
  2. sva::ComBat, batch = source (CCLE / Minna / tumor), mod = ~1,
     ref.batch = Minna. Saved file is bc2 = CCLE_Minna_Thomas_COMBAT.csv
     (not the fsva object).
  3. Fit a NEW PCA on that combined ComBat matrix.
  4. Inverse-transform cell-line PCHA vertices to gene space, then
     pca.transform those vertices. Do not refit PCHA on tumors.
  5. Scatter in combined PC1–PC2; variance: tumor-only PCA ceiling vs
     combined PCs on tumors vs gene-row shuffles.

Breast analogue:
  batches = cell_line (DepMap, n=63) vs tumor (TCGA-BRCA primary, n=1097)
  ref.batch = cell_line
  labels on the scatter = IHC ER/HER2 from the clinical matrix
    breast_carcinoma_estrogen_receptor_status
    lab_proc_her2_neu_immunohistochemistry_receptor_status
  not PAM50, not Panel B LumA/LumB.
