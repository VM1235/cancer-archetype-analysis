# Pre-registered predictions: archetype fit on mouse SCC (GSE288137)

Written 2026-10-10 21:24 IST, before any PCA, PCHA fit or vertex weight was computed on this dataset.
Do not edit this file after the first fit. Add later notes in a separate file.

## Primary analysis (fixed in advance)

- Cells: 23,959 labelled cells from the GEO Seurat object (12 tumours, scRNA + multiome).
- Matrix: `expr_log_filtered.h5ad` (log1p of counts per 10k; genes detected in at least 1% of cells; mito/ribo genes removed; 14,502 genes). No imputation (no MAGIC). No batch correction. Genes mean-centred, not scaled.
- Fit cells: tumour-balanced subsample, at most 800 cells per tumour, seed 0. All other cells are projected into the frozen fit.
- Two gene sets, both reported:
  - `all`: all 14,502 genes.
  - `ks`: Tan 2014 KS epithelial/mesenchymal genes, mapped to mouse symbols. This matches the gene panel used for the Pal breast fits.
- Engine: `src/archetypes.fit_pcha_best` through `host_utils.fit_host_matrix` (PCHA on the first k-1 PCs, delta = 0, 15 inits, keep the largest volume).
- k from 3 to 7. Choose k* as the smallest k with permutation p < 0.01 that also sits at or before the ESV elbow. A significant p alone is not taken as evidence of a simplex.
- Specialist: largest barycentric weight minus second largest is at least 0.35 (same rule as the Pal work).

## Predictions

1. k* = 3 (a triangle).
2. The corners are epithelial, late-EMT and a proliferative/cycling hybrid state. This follows the paper's Slingshot trajectory: an epithelial root with one branch through hybrid to late EMT and another ending in a cycling hybrid state.
3. The `clusterU` label "Proliferative Hybrid EMT" (2,080 cells) is enriched near the third corner.
4. Early-hybrid cells sit on the epithelial edges or in the interior, not at a corner. More early-hybrid cells are interior (purity < 0.35) than epithelial or late-EMT cells.
5. Alternative outcome stated in advance: the third corner may be defined by cell cycle (G2M) and not by an EMT state. Test: G2M enrichment at each corner, and a refit with `phase` regressed out.
6. Risk stated in advance: a corner may be one tumour or one assay. Test: tumour × vertex table, leave-one-tumour-out refits, scRNA-only refit.

## Outcomes that would count against the predictions

- k* > 3, or no k with p < 0.01.
- No corner is enriched for Proliferative Hybrid EMT cells.
- A corner is supplied mostly (over 50%) by one tumour, or disappears when that tumour is left out.
- The cycling corner disappears when cell-cycle phase is regressed out (then it is a cell-cycle task, not an EMT state).
