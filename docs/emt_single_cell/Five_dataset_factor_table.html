Five datasets × eight factors

Everything below is read from the v2 report and the saved output files in the repo. Items marked NOT DONE or estimate are not measured.

How to read "containment". inside % = share of cells whose barycentric weights are all ≥ 0, i.e. lie geometrically inside the fitted simplex (from each fit's describe_k*/summary.md). t-ratio = simplex volume ÷ convex-hull volume of the data; the permutation p tests whether that t-ratio beats shuffled data. Inside % was written out only for SCC, 4T1 and GSE114687 pooled fits; for Cook and PDAC it was not recorded (it can be computed from the saved archetypes).
How corners were named (all datasets): top marker genes of the cells nearest each corner; composition by EMT state, sample, phase; then MSigDB Hallmark gene-set scores at each corner (z of nearest 10% of cells minus the rest).

1. What the dataset means Real mouse skin tumours (LKPR model), 12 tumours, 23,959 cells, with the authors' 4 EMT states. EMT happens in vivo but tumour, assay (scRNA vs multiome) and state are tangled. 4 human cancer lines given TGF-β and sampled over time. Time after TGF-β is an EMT coordinate independent of our score: the cleanest test of time-ordered EMT. One line = one identity. Mouse pancreatic tumour cells from 2 mice at several body sites (primary, liver, lung, metastasis) = metastasis lineage. EMT labels are ours (KS score bins), so partly circular. 5 single-cell clones of 4T1 breast cancer chosen along an epithelial–mesenchymal spectrum, plus parental, one tumour each. Clone = tumour = batch; only clone E is clearly separate on EMT. Human mammary cells grown as colonies, so EMT rises with distance from the colony edge (inner vs outer = "pseudospace"); Mock vs TGF-β. Induced EMT in culture, no replicates.
Gene setShape finding by PCA + PCHA, then a permutation test. "Task corners" = what the corners represent.
2. Hallmark EMT genes
(200-gene MSigDB set)		 NOT DONE in any dataset. Hallmark sets were used only to name corners after fitting (Gap 3 in the report, "unchanged"). Why not: never prioritised; KS genes were chosen first because the earlier breast (Pal) work used them. Needed first: map symbols (human uppercase; mouse by capitalisation, as for KS) and check how many of the 200 are present. Time (estimate, not measured): the gene set is small, so PCA is fast. A pooled run is 5 k-values × 200 shuffles run in parallel, likely tens of minutes per dataset (SCC's all-genes pooled run took hours; KS-size sets are far lighter). Within-sample fits: measured median 12 min each (range 1.5 to 25) across the audit's 104 fits. A pooled fit on all five datasets plus within-sample fits is roughly an afternoon to one day of compute on this machine, plus about a day to write up. Realistic expectation from the KS results: no simplex pooled, because Hallmark EMT overlaps KS heavily. A planted-triangle power check should come with it.

7. Patterns inferred A triangle exists on all genes but its third corner is technology. Without that axis the pooled triangle disappears; two of three mixed tumours still carry one. EMT order is shared across tumours (cross-projection into SC32). Hybrids are generalists. Pooled shape = cell identity. Time ordering is real in A549 and a pseudotime path explains 7 of 8 fits. Corners are TGF-β and proliferation programmes. Middle time point is the most generalist. Pooled blur = mouse and site. Cleaning reveals per-library triangles with epithelial / proliferation / EMT-or-hypoxia corners; EMT is an edge. Corners are mostly not shared across mice. Hybrids are interior. The "spectrum" is one epithelial clone plus five similar ones, so there is little EMT variance to fill a simplex. Corners are cycling and clone M. Cleaning removes within-clone shapes: earlier ones were stress, immune and stromal contamination. The clearest trade-off: cycling vs differentiated vs EMT/matrix, recurring across cell lines and arms and lining up with colony position. Pooled shape is sample identity. EMT is a strong axis (36 to 90% of PC1) with extra dimensions added by cycling and differentiation.
